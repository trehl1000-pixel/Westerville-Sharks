Milestones

  • Spring 2026 power surge: 23 team HR (previous best: 6), 11.2 XBH per 100 PA.
  • Dominic Frate: 10 HR, 1.749 OPS, 1.094 SLG, 6 triples in Spring 2026. Career .527 AVG with 13 HR in 168 PA. Contact rate 65% → 89% → 100%; strikeout rate 31% → 8% → 0%.
  • Chase Rehl: 55 RBI and 21 XBH in Spring 2026; career-high 1.450 OPS this fall. Fly-ball rate 18% → 30% → 56%.
  • Career leaders on this roster: Buss 181 SB and 188 R; Rehl 147 H, 154 RBI and 7 HR; Thomas Jr. .446 AVG; Muller 144 R.
  • Lucas Stanley, Fall 2026: career-best 1.225 OPS, 6.3% K (was 24–31%), 6.0 BB/K.
  • Easton Muller: rebound from .700 OPS (Fall 2025) to 1.391, with 46% line drives.
  • Fielding: Buss, Rehl, Frate, English, Stanley, Bryant and Caterino are all at 1.000 this fall.

Keep on trajectory

  • Strikeout control and quality at-bats. K% 18.6 → 8.7 and QAB% 53 → 68. This is the clearest multi-year trend and it has held through three level jumps.
  • Frate's contact skills and Rehl's loft and hard contact. These two are the engine of the lineup.
  • Stanley's approach (walks up, strikeouts way down) and Muller's line-drive contact.
  • Baserunning: ~95% steal success over 3 years (36 SB in 7 games this fall).
  • Clean glove work: several regulars are error-free so far.

Deviate from

  • Pitching command. The staff has 40 BB in 29.2 IP (WHIP 2.66). Buss: 13 BB and 4 K in 4.1 IP (K/BB 0.31, down from 1.5–4.1). Rehl: K/BB 0.38. Few strikes thrown → zone-first bullpen work.
  • Larken Buss's batting slide. OPS 1.23 → 1.00 → .88, line drives 22% → 8%, ground balls 50%, BABIP .50 → .33. Walks are covering for it (OBP .524).
  • Anthony Thomas Jr.'s short at-bats. QAB 47% (71% in Spring 2025), 2.76 pitches per PA (lowest of anyone in this data), and 1-for-2 on steals.
  • Ground-ball tilt for Buss, Bryant, Caterino and English (46–78% GB). Cameron English has 0 XBH and a 9.5% six-plus-pitch rate this fall.
  • Walk-heavy lineup (26% BB). It lifts OBP, but if it reflects taking hittable strikes, power stays stuck at 7.7 XBH per 100 PA vs. 11.2 last spring.

SWOT

Strengths

  • Elite contact and K-rate (8.7%)
  • Quality AB rate 68%
  • 9-player core with 3+ seasons together
  • Frate / Rehl middle-order power
  • ~95% stolen-base success

Weaknesses

  • Walks allowed (23% of BF)
  • Buss and Thomas Jr. production dip
  • Few hard-hit balls from bottom of order
  • Thin proven pitching depth

Opportunities

  • Pfeifer: best staff line (3.32 ERA, .150 BAA) but 3 HBP and 2 errors
  • Turn 26% walk rate into runs via aggressive-on-strikes plan
  • Develop Caterino and Bryant (strong QAB, fielding)
  • Spring prep to repeat 2026 power

Threats

  • 13U pitchers are faster; hitters like Rehl are being walked (40% BB)
  • Stronger catcher arms vs. a running offense
  • Walk-prone innings extend games and tax arms
  • Small samples: 7 games, 3 new players

Next steps

  1. Strike-throwing plan: bullpen sessions with a target of <1 BB/inning; pitch counts and walk tracking for Buss, Rehl, DePompei, Frate.
  2. Buss: tee and front-toss work on line drives and a lower launch path; check recent mechanics.
  3. Thomas Jr.: two-strike approach, see more pitches, and review steal reads before running.
  4. Hitting plan: "take the first strike only if it isn't your pitch" for the walk-heavy middle of the order.
  5. Use Pfeifer and Stanley as building blocks for more innings; keep Muller as a utility bullpen option.
  6. Re-run this analysis at ~15 games and again after Spring 2027 prep; set targets of K% ≤ 10, QAB% ≥ 65, BB/BF ≤ 15%.
